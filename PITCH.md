# CampusPilot — 90-second pitch

Students don't usually fail because they have nothing to do. They fail because they have too many things competing for attention.

CampusPilot solves that problem with a single student command center.

The first feature is Attendance AI. A student enters attended classes, total classes, classes per day and working days left. CampusPilot calculates the current percentage, recovery potential and the maximum safe number of classes they can miss.

Second is the Adaptive Planner. Instead of making a generic timetable, it considers the student's available study time and energy, then prioritizes high-risk and high-priority work first.

Third is the Task Engine, which keeps assignments in one place and feeds those tasks into the planner.

Finally, Focus Lab turns the recommendation into action with a distraction-free 25-minute sprint.

The prototype is local-first, requires no login or API key, and works directly in a browser.

Our goal is simple: don't just tell students what they have to do. Tell them what they should do next.
